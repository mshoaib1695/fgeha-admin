FGEHA-RSP (FGEHA Resident Service Portal)

Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This page explains how users of the FGEHA-RSP mobile app can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

  • Open the FGEHA-RSP app and go to Profile.
  • Tap Deactivate profile to immediately disable your account access (optional first step).
  • To request permanent deletion, contact FGEHA support / admin using your registered name and phone number or email.
  • We may verify your identity before processing a permanent deletion request.

What data is deleted

  • Personal profile details (such as name, email, phone, house details, profile photo, and ID card images) may be deleted or anonymized.
  • Push notification tokens linked to your account are removed.
  • Login access to the account is permanently disabled.

What data may be kept

  • Operational or audit records (for example service request history) may be retained when required for administration, compliance, or legal obligations.
  • Any retained data is kept only for the minimum period needed for those purposes.

Support

For account deletion, reactivation, or privacy questions, contact the FGEHA system administrator through official FGEHA support channels.
